Objectivity, clarity, and the grid

The Swiss (International) style emphasizes clarity through asymmetric layouts, strong typographic hierarchy (often Helvetica), and rigorous use of a grid. This project documents the history, shows canonical examples, and demonstrates Swiss layout principles in the site itself.

Key characteristics

  • Strong use of grid and alignment
  • Sans-serif typography (neutral, functional)
  • Asymmetric layouts, generous white space
  • Photographic imagery combined with typographic clarity
